        Classes taken this week

        Click a teacher to open their week. Yellow = room left · orange = nearly full · red = over the max.

        Days and max this week

        A factory day = more than 7 sessions of field training (تدريب ميداني) in a day. Its sessions are not counted as classes, and it lowers the teacher's days available by 1. Fewer field-training sessions in a day count as normal classes. Max = 5 classes for each available day (5 days = 25, 4 = 20, 3 = 15); you can change the days or type any max for this week. Taken = planned − absent − given to others (substitutions, not absent) + classes covered + extra classes; Left = max − taken. ⚠ marks a teacher with 6 or more classes on a day. Press Save week to keep changes.
