        · for one class and one teacher, from Excel

        1. Choose the class and the teacher, then download the template: one row per student, one column per date. 2. Fill each cell with the evaluation: a letter (O, P, D, N, U, A), the English word (Outstanding, Proficient, Developing, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory, Absent) or the Arabic one.         Week: absences and substitutions

        Click a day name to make it an absent day, a factory day, or a normal day again. On a factory day (from the timetable or added), click the day name to move it to another teacher or cancel it. A factory day puts the teacher's classes that day under Needs cover and lowers their max by 5; a cancelled one gives the 5 back. Click a class to mark that session only absent; click again to undo. Click a free session (+ extra) to give the teacher an extra class.

        Needs cover

        This week, every teacher

        A factory day = more than 7 sessions of field training (تدريب ميداني) in a day. Its sessions are not counted as classes, and it lowers the teacher's days available by 1. Fewer field-training sessions in a day count as normal classes. Max = 5 classes for each available day (5 days = 25, 4 = 20, 3 = 15); you can change the days or type any max for this week. Total = classes − absent + substitutions + extra classes; Left = max − total. ⚠ marks a teacher with 6 or more classes on a day. Press Save week to keep changes.
